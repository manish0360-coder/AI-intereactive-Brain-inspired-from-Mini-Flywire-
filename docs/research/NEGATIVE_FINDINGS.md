## What the negative record adds up to (auditor's reading; no new analysis)

1. No study in the repository shows the shipped agent learning something hidden and using it. The two preregistered tests of that
   (M7 G15, LEARN-0 E1) came out FAIL and INCONCLUSIVE; the two others (H1-R, ACQ-1) are VOID.
2. The one robust positive line (the goal-entry floor) is a scoring-rule effect. Each record in that line states it is not learning,
   planning or routing capability.
3. Three mechanisms that were built or shipped have no demonstrated benefit: FutureScore V2.3 (UNRESOLVED), the executive controller
   (inert), and persisted `rewards` / `signals` / `confidence` stores (harmful or inert, exploratory).
4. On the separate `cognitive/` testbed the first new mechanism (MBVI) failed its own gate on calibration and one of its declared
   priors was contradicted; its planning advantage over Dyna-Q held on 20 worlds and is development evidence only.
