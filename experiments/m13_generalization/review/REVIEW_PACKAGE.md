## The study in one paragraph

A small testbed: 24-node graphs with hidden edge reliabilities. The agent sees only its node label, its number of
exits and the goal. In task E5, six edges are blocked in turn during a run, and each block reopens with probability
0.1 (RARE), 0.5 (HALF) or 0.9 (OFTEN).

The owner's adopted agent (AD-13, "BELIEF") is a value-iteration planner on world model v6. That model keeps a
two-regime belief about each exit it has seen change, with ρ fixed at 1/2 and hazard h = 0.01. It was developed and
gated (CF-06) on one generator, the development family. The baseline, MBVI3, is the same planner on model v3, with
change detection but no belief about reversal.

**M13 asks:** does BELIEF keep its advantage over MBVI3, with nothing tuned, on a family of worlds structurally unlike
the development family that no agent had seen? The family is modular rings: 4 modules of 6 nodes, a ring of single
bridges, degree at most 4. There are 165 fresh graphs, frozen as a bank before any agent ran.

**The recorded answer** is NET ADVANTAGE RETAINED ON THE MODULAR FAMILY, WITHIN THE SAFETY MARGIN. All five lines
pass.

## Questions

Please answer each with a verdict (sound / sound with caveats / weak / wrong), one to three sentences citing a file
or appendix, and a classification (MATERIAL / MINOR / NOTE) for anything you would change.

1. **Implementation against the specification.** Do `run_m13.mjs`, `m13_lib.mjs` (`analyse`, `readingRule`) and
   `measures.mjs` compute exactly the criteria, the bootstrap seeds, the verdict lines and the summary that SPEC.md
   defines?
2. **Ground-truth isolation.** Is there any path by which the task's ground truth (`context.isBlockedPair`, the
   schedule, edge reliabilities) reaches BELIEF or MBVI3? The diagnostic arms receive it, by design, only as
   `archiveIf` (`cognitive/testbed/arms_v6.mjs`).
3. **Held-out status and the choice of family.** The family was chosen before any agent ran on it, from structure
   alone. But the choice used knowledge from M11 and M12 (self-identified point 1 below). Is "one held-out structural
   family" a fair description? Does the choice bias the comparison in a way the record should state more strongly?
4. **Novelty.** Is the pre-declared novelty check adequate to call the family structurally different? It requires
   the bank's 5th-percentile clustering to exceed the development family's 95th percentile, and cyclomatic number 13
   against 7.
5. **Statistics.**
   - Per-label paired percentile bootstrap (10,000 resamples) on skewed per-label sums: for G1, SD 223 and median 44.
   - n = 165, chosen by power for d = 0.22.
   - Uncorrected secondary criteria.
   - Summing three families per label.

   Is anything mishandled?
6. **Safety (G2).** BELIEF uses more steps than MBVI3 in 133 of 165 RARE labels. The margin of 25 is inherited from
   CF-06. Is "SAFE", and the wording around it, defensible?
7. **The G4 construct.** G4 counts reopened edges an agent met while blocked and crossed again, at any time. It is a
   count, not a rate. BELIEF also meets more blocks (3.32 against 2.31 a run in OFTEN). Does G4 support C4 as worded?
8. **Attribution.** Are A1–A5 and the exactness check computed as specified? Are C5 and its stated limits correct?
9. **Post-result analyses** (RESULTS §8 and §9 and their scripts). Are they computed correctly, labelled as
   post-result, and worded within what they show? Does C6 overreach?
10. **The independent check** (`results/check/verify_m13.mjs`), written before the result existed. Is it independent
    enough to support "all checks pass"? Did it check what RESULTS §1 says it checked?
11. **Provenance and integrity:** hashes, pins, determinism, the clean-tree rule, the wake request and standby log,
    and the disclosed looks before the freeze. Is anything missing or inconsistent?
12. **Conclusions.** Are RESULTS §12 and the PROJECT_STATE wording supported? Is there anything to withdraw or narrow?
13. **Overall:** SOUND / SOUND WITH CORRECTIONS / NOT SOUND. List the MATERIAL findings first.

## Self-identified points

The designer lists these so that the review need not rediscover them. None changed a verdict line under the frozen
rules. Whether any should change the wording is for the reviewer.

1. **The family was chosen with mechanism knowledge.** PROPOSAL.md §2 chose the modular ring partly as
   "mechanism-relevant": a heavier tail of long detours (11% of blocks costing 4+ steps, against 2% in development).
   M12 had found that the gain comes from going back to a written-off route.
   - The perturbed lattice was rejected because its zero-detour share (56%) resembled the app graph's (70%), where
     M11's effects were small, and because it skipped 19 events.
   - No agent ran on either family before the freeze.
   - So the family is held out from development, but it was not drawn at random. It was chosen as structurally
     different and testable, in a direction that M12's account predicts favours BELIEF.
   - RESULTS §11.1–2 state "one family" and "descriptors move together". They do not state this selection.
2. **Same author.** The verifier, the driver and the analyses have one author. The verifier is independent in code
   path only. It shares `pairedBootstrap` (for the interval seeds), `planE5` (the schedule) and the world builder.
3. **G4's raw inputs.** `met` and `crossedLater` are computed from the environment's step log during the run. That
   log is not stored for the 1,962 untraced runs.
   - The pre-result verifier checks those measures for internal consistency, and their own-window parts against the
     task's own counts. It cannot rebuild `met` or `crossedLater`.
   - This package adds a supplementary check, written after the result:
     [check_measures_from_traces.mjs](check_measures_from_traces.mjs). It rebuilds every event's measures in the 18
     traced runs from the recorder's separate step log: all 108 events agree field by field, with 782 ground-truth
     window checks.
   - Run B recomputed the same measures in the 990 core runs, untraced, and matched exactly. That shows determinism,
     not independence.
4. **G4 is a count conditional on meeting.** The per-run conditional rate (met and returned / met) is about 59% for
   BELIEF and 21% for MBVI3 in OFTEN: 1.95 / 3.32 and 0.49 / 2.31, from RESULTS §4. That ratio of means is not a
   pre-declared statistic.
5. **HALF is carried by fewer, larger differences.** Its median is −1, and 84 labels favour MBVI3 against 76 for
   BELIEF. G1 sums the families and is CONSISTENT, but the top decile of labels supplies 51.7% of G1's sum.
6. **The RARE cost is real.** BELIEF is worse in 133 of 165 labels. "SAFE" is relative to CF-06's margin only.
7. **Post-result material.** RESULTS §7 to §9, their scripts and this package's two supplementary scripts were all
   written after the result. The pre-declared illustration (§9) shows no return by either agent. It is reported as it
   came out.
8. **Step scales differ.** M13's runs are about twice as long as M11's. The cross-study raw comparisons in §7 are rough.
   The standardized effect is the only like-for-like number.
9. **Two descriptive checks were adapted from M11 after seeing the result:** the crossing split (§8) and the trace
   summary (§9). §8's two groupings (own window, any time) were both printed and reported.
10. **Diagnostic capped episodes.** BELIEF_REALONLY had one capped episode in RARE and one in OFTEN. No criterion uses
    it. The core arms had none.
11. **The bootstrap tags.** G2 and G4 use the same tags as the per-family table rows for those contrasts. G1 and G3
    have their own. The G3 interval therefore differs from the family-table OFTEN `afterReversed` interval in the
    second decimal.
12. **The proposal's development 95th percentile.** The proposal wrote 0.19, from 60 survey labels. The frozen check
    uses 0.167, computed on the 165 development labels. PROPOSAL.md's status line discloses this.

## Diagnostic arms and attribution: where the definitions are

- **Definition** (SPEC.md, "Attribution"): A1–A5, the "holds" rule (mean > 0 and lower bound > 0), the labels, and
  the exactness check.
- **The arms** (`cognitive/testbed/arms_v6.mjs`, `armAgentV6`): BELIEF with exactly its parameters, plus
  `archiveIf(pairKey)`, a predicate derived from the task context.
  - BELIEF_REALONLY archives an exit that its change detector declares worse only if the task has that edge blocked
    at that moment: a genuine change, reversing or permanent.
  - BELIEF_FALSEONLY archives it only if the edge is open at that moment: a false alarm.
  - Each declaration is classified once, by the state of the world when it is made.
  - The predicate's only consumer is world model v6's archive step (`cognitive/mind/v6/world_model.mjs`,
    `mayArchive`; every exit may be archived when `archiveIf` is absent).
- **Construction** (`m13_lib.mjs` lines 46–49, `ARMS`): BELIEF is built by `makeAdoptedAgent(env, label)` and MBVI3
  by `makeAgentForV6('MBVI3', env, label, {})`. Neither builder takes the context; `runOne` (line 62) passes it as an
  unused third argument.
- **The computation:**
  - the driver: `m13_lib.mjs` lines 172–185, inside `analyse`: contrasts A1–A5, the exactness flag and the
    `gainSource` labels;
  - the independent recomputation: `results/check/verify_m13.mjs`, lines 105–119;
  - the per-label values: `per_label.csv`, columns `A1_…` to `A5_…`.
- **The exactness inputs:** each row's `diagnostics.alarms.opensFalse` / `opensReal`, from task E5's `diagnoseE5`
  (`cognitive/testbed/task_e5.mjs`).
- **Tests:** `cognitive/tests/test_m13_generalization.mjs`, ARMS and PASSIVE.
