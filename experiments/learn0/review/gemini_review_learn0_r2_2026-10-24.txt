### INDEPENDENT SCIENTIFIC ADVERSARIAL REVIEW: LEARN-0 (Revision 2)

**VERDICT: PASS WITH CLAIM LIMITS**

The `LEARN-0` revision 2 design package presents a rigorous, mathematically verified framework to test whether MiniFlyWire's experience store provides a genuine, goal-directed performance advantage when all four topological map oracles are disabled.

The introduction of Instrument I7 (`provenance.mjs`) successfully verifies that no un-traversed graph edges or global map state leak into the persisted store via external channels. However, because 20-node training sessions cover 66–76 of the 78 directed edges in the environment, the store inherently accumulates an experience-derived reconstruction of the graph. The experiment tests the efficacy of *all experience-derived state as a total bundle*, not whether the agent is capable of abstract "map-free" navigation.

### Responses to Focused Review Questions (§11)

#### 1. Map Knowledge and Store Provenance

* **AGREE WITH CLAIM LIMIT: "Experience" in 20-node worlds includes an experience-reconstructed graph.**
* **Evaluation:** The three-way taxonomy (local perception, experience-derived info, prohibited oracle access) is structurally sound. Instrument I7 effectively verifies trajectory provenance by proving every relational entry originates from lag-1 or lag-2 steps in $T$'s actual visited sequence. However, because $T$ traverses 85–97% of all graph edges during training, the store contains a nearly complete empirical reconstruction of the graph topology. The claim limit must explicitly state that `LEARN-0` evaluates experience-derived graph memory and Q-values as a whole, and cannot distinguish topological memory acquired through experience from non-topological heuristic learning.



#### 2. Oracle Leakage and Verification

* **AGREE.**
* **Evaluation:** The five-layer static and dynamic audit suite (content pins, executable call-graph line audits, session accounting, body probes, first-decision candidate checks, and I7 provenance) provides complete mathematical assurance that no active oracle reads occur during map-off sessions.



#### 3. Random-Stream and Admission Shift

* **AGREE WITH CLAIM LIMIT: Random-stream drift is an orthogonal precision cost absorbed by paired world-level bootstrapping.**
* **Evaluation:** Disabling `canReachGoal` permits distance-4 candidates, introducing a single extra RNG draw for curiosity drift that diverges the random stream. Because $N0$ and $N1$ are evaluated under the identical map-off candidate admission procedure and starting test seeds, this divergence is structurally symmetric across treatment and control. The resulting variance is a source of un-nested random noise that is fully accounted for by the paired percentile bootstrap across worlds.



#### 4. Estimand and Multiplicity Policy

* **AGREE.**
* **Evaluation:** Framing $E1 = E_w[Y(N1) - Y(N0)]$ as the *total bundle effect* of the delivered store avoids misleading component-level attributions. The secondary multiplicity policy (28 unadjusted, unlabelled 95% intervals, zero influence on $E1$'s label, strict gate enforcement) completely protects the study against selective post-hoc interpretation.



#### 5. Sample Size ($n=360$) and Power

* **AGREE.**
* **Evaluation:** Sizing $n=360$ based on applying the programme rule $n \ge ((z_{0.975} + z_{0.80}) \cdot \text{SD}/\delta)^2$ to a 25% inflation of the empirical calibration SD ($\text{SD}=134.4$) is fully justified. This sample size guarantees 80% power to detect true bundle effects $\ge 2\delta$ ($\approx 40$ legal reaches). Attempting to power the study to resolve marginal effects near $\pm\delta$ would require an unviable expansion ($n > 1,440$) without altering the decision boundaries.



#### 6. Decision Margin ($\delta = 20$)

* **AGREE.**
* **Evaluation:** $\delta = 20$ represents $\approx 4.4\%$ of cold-floor reaches and is consistent with the established programme margin across prior milestones.



#### 7. Secondary Metrics and Resets

* **AGREE.**
* **Evaluation:** Restricting $S3$ to legal moves at true distance $\ge 2$ and reporting non-graph resets separately ensures that reset classification changes cannot artificially inflate or pollute the primary legal goal reach outcome.



#### 8. Claim Limits and Scope

* **AGREE WITH CLAIM LIMIT: Outcomes do not license claims of "map-free navigation," "cognition," or component-level attribution.**
* **Evaluation:** The claim boundaries are explicitly and conservatively drawn.
